About Yugya

Honest engineering, built from first principles.

Yugya was started in Nepal with a straightforward conviction: businesses, schools, and individuals deserve technology that works reliably, without agency bureaucracy or inflated marketing claims.

We build websites, custom tools, and hardened server setups with clean code, direct engineer access, and complete client ownership.

Why Yugya Exists

Solving practical problems rather than selling buzzwords.

Across Nepal and emerging digital markets, organizations looking to build a digital presence are often caught between two bad options:

On one hand, low-cost marketplace templates that look flashy in a demo but break on mobile devices, take 10 seconds to load, and leave servers vulnerable to automated attacks.

On the other hand, large commercial agencies that charge enterprise retainers for simple websites, hide behind layers of account managers, and lock clients into proprietary hosting systems.

Yugya was created to provide a disciplined middle path: high engineering quality, transparent scoping, direct communication with the developers writing your code, and total client ownership of every asset created.

What We Stand For
  • Clean Semantic Code: We use lean HTML, CSS, and modern JavaScript where appropriate. We don't bloat websites with unnecessary dependencies.
  • No Artificial Lock-in: You own the Git repository, domain accounts, and server credentials outright. We build so you can leave at any time.
  • Accessible & Mobile First: Tested for fast loading across cellular 3G and 4G networks common throughout Nepal.
  • Honest Scoping: If an off-the-shelf free tool solves your problem better than custom development, we will tell you directly.

Our Philosophy

Six operating principles that guide our work.

These guidelines determine how we evaluate project requests, write code, manage servers, and support clients after launch.

01

Clarity before code

We never begin writing code until business goals, target audience, technical constraints, and handover criteria are thoroughly understood and documented.

02

Simplicity over novelty

We select tools that have proven longevity. We resist adding trendy JavaScript frameworks when semantic HTML, clean CSS, and standard APIs do the job faster.

03

Full client ownership

You own 100% of the repository, design assets, domain records, and server credentials. We build systems that any competent engineer can maintain after us.

04

Accessibility as a baseline

High contrast, visible keyboard focus indicators, screen reader landmarks, and responsive mobile layouts are non-negotiable standards on every build.

05

No manufactured claims

We do not invent statistics, fake client numbers, or bogus 99.999% uptime guarantees. We tell you exactly what is tested, verified, and operational.

06

Long-term reliability

We configure automated backups, SSL auto-renewal, and firewall protections so your applications keep working smoothly long after the project ships.
