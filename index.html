Execution Framework

A straightforward, four-stage delivery process.

No endless meetings or hidden retainer scopes. Every engagement follows a disciplined progression from initial problem definition to verified production handover.

01

Discovery & Scoping

We clarify your business goals, target audience, technical constraints, and deadlines. We tell you upfront what is achievable and what is out of scope.

02

Architecture & Design

We define the simplest, most durable architecture. Content hierarchy, wireframes, and database models are reviewed before a line of code is written.

03

Phased Build & Audits

Implementation proceeds in testable milestones with regular demos. Code is checked for accessibility, responsive layout, performance, and clean markup.

04

Verification & Handover

Full deployment to production. You receive 100% of the repository, server documentation, access credentials, and 30 days of post-launch bug support.
