Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before starting.

How does project payment work?

We typically structure engagements in phased milestones: an initial deposit upon project kick-off, a milestone payment after architecture review, and the final payment upon successful production deployment and code handover.

Do you host our website on your own servers?

No. We configure servers under your own accounts (e.g. DigitalOcean, Linode, or Cloudflare). You own the hosting account directly and pay the provider directly at true cost. We never hold your infrastructure hostage.

What if we need changes after launch?

Every build includes 30 days of bug fixing support at zero extra charge. For ongoing feature development or monthly security patching, we offer predictable monthly maintenance agreements.

Can you help register a .com.np domain?

Yes. In Nepal, .np domains are provided free of cost by the official registry (Mercantile), but require specific corporate registration and cover letter documents. We prepare the complete paperwork dossier for you.
