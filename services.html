Service Offerings & Scopes

Defined capabilities, itemized deliverables.

We do not sell vague retainers or buzzword packages. Every Yugya engagement is structured around an explicit operational problem, a concrete deliverable, and documented handover.

Below is a transparent breakdown of what we build, who needs it, what the project deliverable includes, and what happens once the project is shipped.

SERVICE 01

Website & Web Application Development

Directly Available

What it is & Who needs it

Custom web development for businesses, schools, and organizations that need a high-performance digital front door or interactive web tool. Ideal for organizations whose current website is slow, broken on mobile devices, or trapped inside rigid, expensive website builders.

The Problem it solves

Most business websites fail because they bury crucial actions behind excessive animations, stock photos, and slow-loading frameworks. We build clean, lightweight sites that load instantly even on 3G cellular connections and make contacting or transacting straightforward.

What the deliverable includes

  • Semantic HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript codebase without heavy third-party baggage
  • Fully responsive layouts tested across small mobile (375px), modern mobile (390px), tablet, and desktop
  • Bilingual architecture support (e.g. English + Nepali toggles) where required
  • Accessible navigation, forms, and keyboard focus states (WCAG AA compliant)
  • Technical SEO foundations: canonical URLs, OpenGraph social cards, Schema.org JSON-LD
  • Complete Git repository ownership transferred to your team

What happens after delivery?

We deploy the site to your production server or static hosting platform, provide a simple walkthrough of how to edit content, and include 30 calendar days of bug fixing support at zero extra charge.

SERVICE 02

Linux Server Setup, VPS & Deployment Pipelines

Directly Available

What it is & Who needs it

Configuring and locking down virtual private servers (VPS) on cloud providers like DigitalOcean, Linode, Hetzner, or AWS. Perfect for teams outgrowing restrictive shared hosting who need dedicated resources, stability, and control.

The Problem it solves

Shared hosting has arbitrary file limits and slow execution speeds, while default Linux servers leave root password logins enabled and firewalls disabled. We build a clean, secure, production-grade Linux environment that protects your applications and automates deployments.

What the deliverable includes

  • OS provisioning (Ubuntu LTS or Debian) with non-root sudo users and SSH key-only access
  • UFW firewall configuration and Fail2ban intrusion prevention
  • High-performance reverse proxy (Nginx or Caddy) with automated Let's Encrypt SSL/TLS certificates
  • Automated deployment pipeline using Git hooks or GitHub Actions CI/CD
  • Systemd service configuration for automated process management and crash recovery
  • Documented server runbook and backup routine instructions

What happens after delivery?

All SSH keys, server root passwords, and cloud dashboard accesses are retained solely by you. We provide a concise Markdown runbook explaining how to start, restart, and inspect service logs.

SERVICE 03

Domain Guidance & DNS Architecture

Directly Available

What it is & Who needs it

Assistance with registering national ccTLDs (specifically Nepal's official .np domains), purchasing global commercial domains (.com, .org), and establishing resilient DNS routing.

The Problem it solves

In Nepal, registering a .com.np domain requires strict documentation (company registration, official cover letters, tax verification) that often gets rejected. Additionally, poorly configured DNS leads to broken emails, missing SPF/DKIM records, and spoofing vulnerabilities.

What the deliverable includes

  • Official Nepal .np application paperwork audit and cover letter preparation
  • DNS routing setup on Cloudflare or provider of choice with DNSSEC activation
  • Email authentication records: MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC for Google Workspace, Zoho, or custom mail
  • Subdomain mapping for web applications, APIs, and staging environments
  • Zero downtime DNS cutover planning and validation

What happens after delivery?

Your domain account remains in your direct legal ownership. We deliver an export of all active DNS records and configuration steps so any engineer can maintain it in the future.

SERVICE 04

Business Automation & Custom Tooling

Scoped Engagement

What it is & Who needs it

Custom lightweight scripts, automated data sync pipelines, and local utility tools written in Python or Node.js. For business teams spending hours manually copying data between spreadsheets, forms, or messaging channels.

The Problem it solves

Repetitive manual entry introduces data corruption, delays customer responses, and consumes productive engineering or administrative hours. We automate these workflows with transparent, maintainable scripts.

What the deliverable includes

  • Process discovery: documenting input sources, transformation rules, and output destinations
  • Standalone Python/Node.js script or automated webhook handler with clear error logging
  • Local SQLite or CSV/JSON storage pipeline with automated backups
  • Automated alert mechanism (Telegram, email, or Slack webhook) on failure or completion
  • Step-by-step operating guide and cron schedule configuration

What happens after delivery?

We run test batches together with your team, demonstrate edge-case failure handling, and provide instructions on how to adjust parameters as your business rules evolve.

SERVICE 05

Ongoing Maintenance & Technical Care

Directly Available

What it is & Who needs it

Monthly operational checkups and proactive maintenance for deployed websites and servers. For businesses without full-time internal engineering teams that need someone reliable to keep things working.

The Problem it solves

Web applications rot when neglected. Unrenewed SSL certificates cause browser warnings, unpatched server packages introduce security holes, and unmonitored disk space causes unexpected outages.

What the deliverable includes

  • Monthly server OS security patching and software package upgrades
  • SSL certificate health checks and DNS renewal monitoring
  • Automated offsite database and asset backup verification
  • Minor content updates and broken link repair
  • Direct engineer contact via email or dedicated chat for technical emergencies

What happens after delivery?

At the end of every month, you receive a concise summary report detailing all updates performed, backup integrity checks, and recommendations for improvements.
